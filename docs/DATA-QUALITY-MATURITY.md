# Data Quality Maturity

| Level | Name | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initial | Critical metadata is incomplete and ownership is unclear |
| 2 | Repeatable | Some fields and controls exist but are inconsistently maintained |
| 3 | Defined | Mandatory metadata, ownership and standards are documented |
| 4 | Managed | Quality is measured, governed and actively remediated |
| 5 | Optimised | Quality controls are automated and continuously improved |

Dimensions: completeness, validity, consistency, uniqueness, timeliness, traceability and stewardship.
