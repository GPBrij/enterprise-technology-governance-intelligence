# Decision Rules

| Trigger | Risk points | Required response |
|---|---:|---|
| End of life, near end of life or no support | 25 | Migration or retirement roadmap |
| Support end within 12 months or past | 15 | Extended support or accelerated replacement |
| Maturity below Level 3 | 15 | Complete and govern metadata |
| Missing business or technology owner | 15 | Assign accountable owners |
| Architecture approval absent | 20 | Submit for architecture approval |
| Personal information without classification | 10 | Privacy classification and impact assessment |
| Incident count above showcase threshold | 10 | Problem and technology health review |

Decision bands: 0-24 Approve; 25-44 Review Required; 45-69 Conditionally Approve; 70-100 Reject and Replace.
