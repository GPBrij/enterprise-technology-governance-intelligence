# Contributing

This is a proprietary, source-available portfolio project. External
contributions are not accepted automatically.

Before submitting code, documentation, designs, issues containing substantial
solution content, or pull requests, contact the project owner at
`pbrijraj@goalpostbrij.co.za`.

A separate Contributor Licence Agreement may be required. Submission of a
contribution does not transfer ownership of the project and does not grant the
contributor or any other party a right to use the project commercially.

Do not submit confidential information, personal information, credentials,
client material, proprietary standards, or content that the contributor does
not have the right to provide.
